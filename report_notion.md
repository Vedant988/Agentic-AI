# Competitive‑Landscape Brief  
**Target:** **Notion** – Productivity‑software platform  
**Key Competitors (as identified):** Airtable, Craft Agents, Scribe  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Market Position:** Notion sits at the intersection of note‑taking, knowledge‑base, and low‑code database. Its **unified‑workspace** model differentiates it from siloed competitors (Airtable = database‑first; Craft = design‑focused docs; Scribe = AI‑driven workflow capture).  
- **Growth Levers:** Recent external shock – Google’s shutdown of *Tables* – creates a potential migration tide toward Notion’s relational‑database blocks.  
- **Risks:** Public perception is **poor** (Trustpilot ≈ 2.2/5) and the pricing matrix is **incomplete** in the source material, making positioning against rivals ambiguous.  
- **Strategic Imperative:** Tighten brand sentiment (customer success, community outreach) **and** publish a clear, tiered pricing roadmap to capitalize on the emerging “spreadsheet‑to‑knowledge‑base” migration.
